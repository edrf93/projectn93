Ads.txt Implementation

Authorized Digital Sellers (ads.txt) is an IAB initiative to increase transparency in programmatic advertising. Follow the guide below to update your file.

1. Copy the Lines

Add the following lines to your ads.txt file hosted at yourdomain.com/ads.txt

nuclion.io, [YOUR_ID], DIRECT, [CERT_ID]
openx.com, XXXXX, RESELLER, XXXXXX
rubiconproject.com, XXXXX, RESELLER, XXXXXX
appnexus.com, XXXXX, RESELLER, XXXXXX

2. Implementation Check

Ensure there are no hidden characters or formatting issues. After updating, our system will automatically crawl your site within 24 hours.

Note: Replacing [YOUR_ID] with your actual publisher ID provided in your dashboard is mandatory.
